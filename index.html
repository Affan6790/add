BOAT (Best Player Of All Time)

Cristiano Ronaldo has officially been crowned the first-ever winner of the inaugural B.O.A.T. (Ballon d'Or of All Time) award, a special public poll launched by France Football to celebrate the greatest individual campaign in football history. Unlike the traditional Ballon d'Or decided by journalists, this historic vote allowed global football fans to choose the ultimate single-season performance from a legendary shortlist of multiple-time award winners like Johan Cruyff, Michel Platini, and Ronaldo Nazário. The voting heavily captured the sport's iconic modern rivalry, seeing Cristiano Ronaldo's unforgettable 2007–2008 season with Manchester United secure the top spot ahead of Lionel Messi's record-breaking 2012 campaign. During that magical 2008 run, Ronaldo scored a staggering 42 goals in all competitions, leading Manchester United to a glorious double by conquering both the English Premier League and the UEFA Champions League. Even though Messi holds more total standard Ballon d'Or trophies overall, the passionate global fanbase overwhelmingly voted Ronaldo's historic English breakthrough as the benchmark of footballing perfection, cementing his legacy as the ultimate B.O.A.T. winner.
